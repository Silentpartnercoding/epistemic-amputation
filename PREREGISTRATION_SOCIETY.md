# Stage-six preregistration: social transmission after epistemic amputation

Status: frozen before the first full stage-six run.

## Question

When the single evidential ancestor of a belief is invalidated, which social
structures make the belief or its associated ritual persist: duplicate copying,
prestige, coordination value, or public commitment?

## Simulation

Forty agents receive one false-positive root observation in a high-uncertainty
hunting environment.  Reports spread through a fixed directed network.  Every
report carries an immutable lineage identifier, allowing a provenance-aware
condition to count the entire cascade once and a naive condition to count every
copy as independent corroboration.

The factorial conditions independently enable prestige weighting, real ritual
coordination value, and costly public commitment.  After formation, an
authoritative message invalidates the root and agents receive independent null
outcomes.  No condition gives the ritual a causal effect on mammoth availability.

## Separation of outcomes

The assay records:

- private causal belief;
- public ritual behavior;
- the number of independent evidential lineages;
- the precommitted defeater threshold and any post-commitment threshold shift;
- transmission and recovery time after root invalidation.

Continued ritual with corrected private belief is classified as coordination or
commitment persistence, not a phantom belief.  A social phantom requires private
causal belief to remain above 0.70 after the root is invalidated and after five
independent null observations.

## Frozen hypotheses

- Provenance awareness reduces false private belief by at least 0.20 compared
  with naive duplicate counting.
- Prestige increases initial spread but cannot independently satisfy the social
  phantom criterion once lineage is deduplicated.
- Coordination value can preserve ritual behavior while private causal belief
  falls below 0.30.
- Public commitment increases criterion migration, operationalized as at least
  one additional independent defeater required after commitment.
- The full social-phantom criterion, if present, must occur in at least 80% of
  50 deterministic society seeds within a condition.

This is a transparent mechanism simulation.  It can distinguish explanations
and generate predictions; it cannot by itself establish that human religion or
an unmodified language model uses any simulated mechanism.
