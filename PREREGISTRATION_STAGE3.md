# Stage-three preregistration: causally ordered world-model assay

Status: frozen after the stage-two null and before the first stage-three run.

## Why this is a new experiment

Stage two found a perfectly decodable sensorimotor body representation but no
phantom conflict and no effective causal patch. Its controller consumed the
current response before producing the action readout, and its patch was applied
before that recurrent update. A fresh observation could therefore overwrite
the intervention. That null remains frozen; stage three corrects the temporal
question rather than changing the prior result.

## Causal ordering

At each step:

1. the controller chooses an allocation from the recurrent body state produced
   by all *prior* evidence;
2. the report head answers using that same state plus the current declaration;
3. the environment returns generic command/response evidence; and
4. the recurrent state updates for the next decision.

This is ordinary action-before-outcome ordering. No morphology mask or explicit
availability variable enters the controller. Effector identity is balanced.

## Intervention

Independent stable-body episodes estimate an absent-minus-present hidden-state
direction for each effector. At the first decision after genuine negative
sensor evidence, the direction is added immediately before the action head.
The same declaration, current environment, weights, and action rule are held
fixed. The reverse intervention and eight deterministic, norm-matched
orthogonal directions are controls.

## Frozen gates

Five model seeds and all three effector identities are evaluated. `SUPPORTED`
requires every gate:

- stable-body learned competence improves unavailable-action mass by 0.15;
- truthful absence is reported with at least 95% accuracy and reduces cold-
  absent unavailable-action mass by 0.08 versus intact;
- after the first real zero-response observation, truthful reporting coexists
  with at least 0.08 excess unavailable-action mass versus cold absence for
  three consecutive decisions;
- the absent-state patch reduces unavailable-action mass by 0.08;
- the reverse patch increases it by 0.08;
- the intended patch is at least twice the median absolute orthogonal-control
  effect; and
- the conflict and both patch directions hold for at least four of five seeds.

An intervention-boundary action emitted before any negative sensor evidence is
expected and cannot count toward persistence. Thresholds will not be changed
after results are observed.

The strongest allowed conclusion remains a learned, belief-like causal body
schema in this assay. No subjective belief, sensation, pain, consciousness, or
biological equivalence is tested.
